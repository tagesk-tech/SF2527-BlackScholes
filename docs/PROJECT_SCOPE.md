# Project scope and presentation contract

## What must be submitted

The examination consists of an oral presentation plus three submitted items:

1. presentation slides as a PDF;
2. computer code;
3. a list of individual contributions.

The talk is at most 15 minutes, every group member must speak, and the MATLAB
source should be submitted but not shown line by line during the seminar.

## Required content

| Rubric item | Evidence in this repository |
|---|---|
| Application problem | Option pricing, payoff, hedging motivation, and why the half-line creates a far-field problem. |
| Mathematical model | Backward Black--Scholes PDE; time-to-maturity transformation; initial payoff; left boundary; degenerate parabolic classification; model assumptions and limitations. |
| Discretization and method | Nonuniform finite differences, the two sequential sweeps, averaging, shrinking active grid, algebraic structure, expected accuracy, and implementation difficulties. |
| Validation | Correct analytical call, digital-call, and put prices and Greeks; comparison with paper tables and profiles. |
| Verification | Separated time and space refinements, multiple error measures, stability/failure records, put--call parity, and planted fault. |
| Discussion | No prescribed right value does not mean an infinite domain; nonsmooth payoffs complicate order; the stability and cost claims have a narrower tested scope than the paper suggests. |
| Rate the paper | Give a numerical rating only after stating correctness, reproducibility, exposition, novelty, evidence, and limitations. |
| Challenges | Off-by-one active-domain indexing, opposite-sweep dependence, benchmark error in the paper, and separating time, space, and moving-domain effects. |

## Minimal work packages

The chat currently names four work packages, not verified person assignments:

1. implement the scheme;
2. validation and spatial convergence;
3. comparison with Jeong et al. (2018);
4. put-option extension.

The repository makes the packages composable: one solver feeds all experiments,
and each result is generated by a named script. The contribution list should be
filled by the group from actual work performed.

## Presentation guardrails

- Say "compatible with second order over the tested range," not "proved second order."
- Distinguish the standard exact call price from the paper's printed benchmark.
- Do not call the two one-dimensional sweeps a multidimensional ADI split.
- Report all failed runs and non-finite outputs in the stability study.
- Separate paper-reported facts, reproduced outputs, and group interpretation.
